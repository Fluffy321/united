Who we cut for

Built around the community

Both shops close early Erev Shabbos and stay closed all day Saturday — then open a full day Sunday, when fathers and sons finally have the time to come in together.

It's a small thing that tells you who the shop is for. Nobody has to explain why they can't come Saturday, and nobody's rushing you out the door before candle lighting.
