What you're getting

This isn't New York pizza.

Connecticut thin-crust — apizza, if you're from New Haven — is its own thing. The dough goes in thin and comes out blistered and charred at the edges, with a chew you don't get from a soft, foldable slice.

It's the pizza people drive across state lines for up north. Now it's here, and it's kosher.

  • 01Charred, not burntThose black blisters on the crust are the whole point — that's where the flavour is.
  • 02Ultra-thin, properly chewyThin enough to hold its shape, never cracker-dry.
  • 03Extra virgin olive oil, full stopNo seed oils on the menu — not in the dough, not on the pie.
  • 04ORB certified kosher dairyUnder supervision, so you don't have to ask.
