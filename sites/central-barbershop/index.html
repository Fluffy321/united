Ask for it by name

Cuts with halachic considerations

Plenty of shops will give you a good haircut. Fewer know what to do when a customer says his son's peyos stay, or that the corners aren't to be touched with a razor.

That's ordinary here. Say what you keep and it gets kept — no long explanation, no awkward conversation in the chair, no coming home to a surprise.

  • Peyos respected — length and placement exactly as you ask
  • Corners of the head — cut in line with what you keep
  • Upsherin — the first cut, handled with the patience a three-year-old needs
  • No explaining required — the staff already know the questions
