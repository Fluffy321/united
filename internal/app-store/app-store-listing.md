## App Name (max 30 chars)

**Recommended:** `JUnited`  (7 chars)

Other options considered:
- `JUnited: Jewish Community` (25 chars) — more search surface area, but redundant with the subtitle below.
- `JUnited — Jewish Life` (21 chars)

**Why "JUnited" alone:** the name is already a strong, ownable brand term. App Store search weighs the *keywords* field for discovery — burning subtitle space repeating "Jewish community" in both the name and subtitle is redundant. Keep the name short and memorable; let the subtitle carry the value proposition.

## Subtitle (max 30 chars)

**Recommended:** `Your Jewish Community, Daily` (29 chars)

Other options considered:
- `Connect. Give. Belong.` (23 chars) — punchy but vague, doesn't say "Jewish"
- `Communities, Chesed & Events` (29 chars) — more feature-literal, less warm

**Why the recommended option:** it names the audience ("Jewish") immediately, which matters for search and for a user scanning results, and "Daily" signals this is a habit app (feed-driven), not a one-time-use utility.

## Keywords (max 100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces after commas, singular forms, no repeats from name/subtitle)

**Recommended string (99 chars):**
```
shul,synagogue,chesed,mitzvah,jewish community,tzedakah,volunteer,neighbor,minyan,local events
```

**Reasoning for top picks:**
1. `shul` / `synagogue` — the two most common terms people search for their place of worship; covers both Yiddish-inflected and formal English search behavior.
2. `chesed` / `mitzvah` — core differentiating features (the volunteer/favor system) and terms the target audience searches in Hebrew/Yiddish transliteration, which competitor "generic community app" listings won't rank for.
3. `jewish community` — direct category match; higher competition but essential.
4. `tzedakah` / `volunteer` — chesed-adjacent terms broadening the volunteer-intent search surface.
5. `neighbor` / `minyan` / `local events` — long-tail, lower-competition terms matching specific use cases (local help, prayer quorum coordination, event discovery).

Excluded: "app", "free" (low value, wastes characters), anything already in the name/subtitle ("jewish" appears in "jewish community" only once to stay under budget — do not also add a bare "jewish" keyword, it's redundant).
