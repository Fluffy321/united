> JUnited is a community platform for Jewish communities: members join one or more communities (synagogues, schools, organizations, or neighborhoods) and use a shared feed, a volunteer/favor board ("Mitzvah Circle"), events, and direct messaging to stay connected.
>
> **Demo account:** appreviewer@junited.us / [DEMO PASSWORD]
> This account is pre-joined to a demo community with existing posts, a Mitzvah request, and an upcoming event, so all features are visible immediately without further setup.
>
> **Suggested testing flow:**
> 1. Sign in with the demo account (or tap "Continue with Apple" / "Continue with Google" — both are available on the login screen alongside email/password).
> 2. **Feed tab** — scroll the community feed, open a post, tap the "..." menu on a post to see Report/Block options, tap into Replies to see comments (also reportable via the flag icon).
> 3. **Mitzvah Circle tab** — view an existing help request; note the "offer to help" flow.
> 4. **Communities tab** — the demo account is already a member of a community; open it to see the community feed, member list, and events.
> 5. **Map tab** — browse nearby communities and local business listings.
> 6. **Messages** — accessible via the message icon in the header; the demo account has no pre-seeded conversations, but you can message another member from their profile.
> 7. **Settings** — tap the avatar/profile icon, then Settings, to see notification preferences, privacy controls, and the account-deletion flow (Settings → Account → Danger Zone → "Delete my account"). Deletion is fully in-app: type "DELETE" to confirm, and the account and its data are removed immediately — no email or support ticket required.
>
> **Content moderation:** Every post, comment, and user profile has a Report option, reachable via the "..." menu (posts/profiles) or the flag icon (comments). Reports are reviewed by community and platform admins. Users can also block other members from a profile's "..." menu; blocked users' content is hidden from the blocker's feed, and blocked users cannot start new conversations with each other.
>
> **Community structure:** most content is scoped to communities a member has joined. The demo account is pre-joined to one so reviewers see real content immediately; new users are guided through onboarding to join a community on first launch.
>
> **Sign-in with Apple** is available on the login screen alongside Google Sign-In and email/password, per Guideline 4.8.
>
> Thank you for reviewing JUnited!
